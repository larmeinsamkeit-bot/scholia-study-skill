# ECON 101 · Midterm 2025 (past paper) — covers Weeks 1–2

Time: 50 minutes · Section A 40% (multiple choice) · Section B 60% (short problems)

## Section A
1. The opportunity cost of going to university includes: (a) tuition only (b) tuition and the income
   you give up (c) the cost of food you would eat anyway (d) sunk costs of past courses.
2. A rise in the price of tea, a substitute for coffee, causes: (a) a movement along the coffee demand
   curve (b) the coffee demand curve to shift right (c) the coffee supply curve to shift left
   (d) no change.
3. If demand is inelastic, a price increase will: (a) raise total revenue (b) lower total revenue
   (c) leave total revenue unchanged (d) lower quantity to zero.

## Section B
4. Country X can make 10 phones or 5 laptops per worker-day; Country Y can make 6 phones or 6 laptops.
   Which country should specialise in laptops? Show the opportunity costs.
5. Qd = 80 − 4P and Qs = 8 + 2P. Find the equilibrium price and quantity.
6. Price falls from $10 to $8 and quantity demanded rises from 50 to 70. Using the midpoint method,
   compute the price elasticity of demand and say whether demand is elastic.

Examiner's note: most marks were lost in Section B for not showing the opportunity-cost working and for
using the simple (not midpoint) percentage change.
